Section 03

Payment and billing protocols are designed to maintain full financial and statutory transparency:

  • Commercial Currencies: Invoices are denominated in Indian Rupees (INR) for domestic supply across India, and in United States Dollars (USD), Euros (EUR), or UAE Dirhams (AED) for international and SAIF Zone Sharjah export consignments.
  • Statutory Duties & Taxes: Domestic prices are exclusive of Goods and Services Tax (GST) unless indicated as GST-inclusive. For international shipments, prices follow agreed Incoterms (e.g. FOB, CIF, Ex-Works), with destination customs clearance, import tariffs, and local municipal charges payable by the overseas importer.
  • Payment Schedules for Equipment Orders: Standard terms require 50% mobilization advance with the Purchase Order, and the balance 50% payable against proforma invoice prior to dispatch from our METL Jhajjar manufacturing plant or warehouse hubs.
  • Payment Schedules for Turnkey Hospital Projects: Turnkey infrastructure, Modular OT, and MGPS contracts follow milestone billing: 40% advance with project commissioning sign-off; 40% upon readiness and dispatch of structural panels, gas pipelines, and primary machinery; 15% upon completion of physical installation; and 5% against final performance testing and handover.
  • Late Payment Interest: Overdue payments beyond agreed credit limits shall accrue interest at 18% per annum, calculated daily from the due date until full realization, without prejudice to our right to suspend ongoing installation or warranty service.

5. Medical Gas Pipeline Systems (MGPS) & Oxygen Plant Standards

Section 05

Medical Gas Pipeline Systems (MGPS) are critical life-support networks. We manufacture, install, and service gas outlets, manifold systems, semi-automatic changeover control panels, bed head units, and PSA oxygen generation plants:

  • Gas Standard Conformity: Gas outlets and terminal units are manufactured to international standards—including BS Standard (Amber), PB Standard (Sapphire), Parkodex (Hook Loose Type), and DIN Standard (Jasper) with front-loaded probes, vacuum regulators, and Avsu zonal valve boxes.
  • Pressure Testing & Purging Protocols: Gas pipelines undergo pneumatic pressure retention testing at specified test bars (Nitrogen purge) for a mandatory 24-hour observation window to ensure zero cross-contamination and absolute joint integrity before medical gases (O2, N2O, Medical Air, Vacuum) are introduced.
  • Safety & Operational Prohibition: Hospital staff must operate gas pipelines strictly within rated operating parameters. Any unauthorized bypass of digital alarm panels, modification of pressure reducing valves, or non-certified cylinder hookups voids all manufacturer liability and warranty immediately.
