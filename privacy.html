2. Categories of Information We Collect

Section 02

To provide hospital-grade infrastructure, precision surgical instruments, turnkey biomedical installations, and patient healthcare services, we collect and process information categorized as follows:

A. Institutional, Hospital & B2B Commercial Data

  • Institutional Representatives: Name, professional title, hospital/clinic name, government or private health enterprise registration details, medical license numbers, official email addresses, and direct phone lines.
  • Turnkey Project Specifications: Architectural blueprints, civil layouts, CAD drawings, MEP schematics, Modular Operating Theatre (OT) volume requirements, and Medical Gas Pipeline System (MGPS) design specifications.
  • Financial & Billing Data: GSTIN/PAN identification, bank remittance details, Letters of Credit (LC) for export consignments, billing and delivery addresses for hospital furniture and machinery.

B. MediAid Patient & Clinical Health Information (Sensitive Personal Data)

  • Personal Identity: Patient full name, age, date of birth, gender, residential address, and emergency contact details.
  • Clinical Diagnostic Records: Medical histories, physician consultation notes, pathology test results, radiology investigations (CT scans, MRI scans, digital mammography, X-rays, ultrasound reports), and operative summaries.
  • Prescription & Home Healthcare Records: Doctor prescriptions transmitted for doorstep medicine delivery or home care assistance (including home nursing, elderly care, and stroke/paralysis physiotherapy).
  • Medical Travel & Concierge Data: Passport copies, international medical visa documentation, arrival flight itineraries, and airport transfer coordinates for foreign patients travelling to partner super-speciality hospitals in India.

C. Technical & Operational Telemetry Data

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address, approximate geographic location, device hardware specs, browser version, access timestamps, visited catalogue categories (e.g. ICU beds, anesthesia workstations, autoclaves), and inquiry form logs.

3. Purpose & Lawful Grounds of Data Processing

Section 03

Under the Indian Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), the Information Technology Act, 2000 (and its Sensitive Personal Data Rules, 2011), and the UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on Personal Data Protection (PDPL), we process personal and institutional data based on lawful grounds:

  • Contractual Performance: Manufacturing hospital furniture, supplying ICU beds, installing medical gas pipeline networks, delivering modular cleanrooms, executing turnkey hospital projects, and honoring one-year free service warranties and Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC).
  • Emergency Healthcare & Patient Assistance: Dispatching air, road, or train ambulances; providing medical equipment on rent (ventilators, BiPAP/CPAP, cardiac monitors, oxygen concentrators); and facilitating timely hospital admissions.
  • Medical Device Regulatory Compliance: Maintaining device traceability, batch records, calibration logs, and safety records as mandated by ISO 13485:2016 and applicable medical device directives.
  • Customer Support & Technical Consultation: Responding to RFQs, product inquiries, providing 365-day dedicated sales and service assistance, and resolving warranty claims across our regional service centers.

4. MediAid Patient Data & Clinical Confidentiality

Section 04

Our healthcare assistance division, MediAid (MediAid Curatives India Pvt. Ltd.), acts as a trusted bridge between domestic/international patients and premier medical institutions. Because medical records constitute sensitive health information, we implement strict healthcare confidentiality standards:

  • Strict Doctor-Patient Confidentiality: Clinical diagnostics, surgical histories, and diagnostic imaging files are accessible only to qualified medical coordinators and attending super-speciality physicians.
  • No Commercial Monetization: We strictly uphold an absolute ban on commercial exploitation. Dr. Smith and MediAid NEVER sell, license, rent, or trade patient health records, diagnostic tests, or prescriptions to third-party advertisers, pharmaceutical companies, or data brokers.
  • Prescription Upload Verification: Prescriptions submitted via WhatsApp or digital forms for medicine delivery or home equipment rental are processed through licensed pharmacists and certified biomedical handlers solely for verification and dispensing.
  • Informed Consent for Teleconsultation: Video consultations and second opinion dossiers with specialist doctors are initiated only upon explicit voluntary authorization by the patient or their legal guardian.

7. Technical, Quality & Biomedical Security Safeguards

Section 07

We deploy a defense-in-depth security infrastructure aligned with our certified Quality Management Systems (ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 13485:2016):

  • Cryptographic Data Protection: All web submissions on drsmith.co.in are protected with 256-bit TLS/SSL encryption in transit. Internal file servers storing hospital designs and patient histories utilize encrypted volumes.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Access to technical drawings, hospital CAD files, and patient clinical records is restricted strictly to authorized project managers, biomedical engineers, and licensed healthcare coordinators.
  • Physical Security Controls: Perimeter security, biometric access, CCTV monitoring, and restricted access zones are maintained at our Model Economic Township (METL) manufacturing facility in Jhajjar and corporate offices in Kapashera and Daryaganj.
  • Biomedical Integrity: Turnkey installations (Modular OTs, Medical Gas Pipeline Systems) undergo stringent quality validation to prevent unauthorized tampering or electronic safety overrides.

8. Data Retention, Traceability & Archival Policies

Section 08

We retain personal and commercial data strictly for the period necessary to fulfill its legitimate operational and legal purposes:

  • Medical Device Traceability Records: In adherence to ISO 13485:2016 standards, equipment serial records, warranty certifications, calibration test sheets, and inspection audits (e.g. for ICU beds, autoclaves, OT tables, anesthesia machines) are archived for the designed operational lifetime of the device.
  • Hospital Construction & Turnkey Engineering Files: Civil schematics, gas manifold layout designs, and modular cleanroom specs are retained for the lifetime of the installation to support future expansion, repairs, and statutory safety audits.
  • Commercial & Tax Records: Invoices, bills of lading, and export declarations are retained for 8 years in compliance with Indian corporate, GST, and international customs regulations.
  • Patient Assistance Records: Medical travel and coordination records are securely archived post-discharge and permanently deleted upon patient request or expiration of follow-up cycles, subject to applicable medical law.

9. Your Rights & Data Subject Empowerments

Section 09

Whether you are an institutional hospital procurement director, a medical practitioner, or a private patient, you are entitled to comprehensive rights under the DPDP Act 2023 and UAE PDPL:

  • Right of Access & Summary: You may request a summary of personal information processed by Dr. Smith, including identities of healthcare partners with whom records were shared.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: You have the right to request correction of inaccurate corporate/contact records and deletion of personal data when no longer needed for warranty or regulatory mandates.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may revoke consent for receiving marketing newsletters, catalogues, or non-essential communications at any time.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: You have direct access to our Data Protection Officer for prompt resolution of any privacy concerns or compliance queries.

10. Cookie Policy & Digital Web Analytics

Section 10

Our digital platform utilizes cookies and similar lightweight technologies to deliver a fast, secure, and user-friendly experience:

  • Essential System Cookies: Critical for navigation, mobile menu rendering, catalogue loading, and secure inquiry form submissions.
  • Functional & Preference Cookies: Remembers your view preferences and device parameters across catalogue categories.
  • Performance & Diagnostics: Anonymized analytical cookies help us evaluate page load speeds, dead link prevention, and server health. We do not use intrusive third-party cross-site behavioral tracking scripts.
