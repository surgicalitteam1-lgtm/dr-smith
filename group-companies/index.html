Our Strategic Group Companies

Each entity operates with domain expertise to provide comprehensive coverage across the medical value chain.

Surgihub

Surgical Devices Hub • Delhi, India

Surgihub acts as a central access point for certified surgical instruments, specialty disposables, laparoscopy kits, and operating theatre essentials across Northern India and national supply chains.

  • Specialized surgical devices and consumable supply
  • Strategic logistics hub located in Delhi, India
  • Rapid fulfillment for clinics, hospitals & distributor networks

SMPL (Surgical Mall of India Pvt. Ltd.)

Comprehensive Trading • Delhi, India

Surgical Mall of India Pvt. Ltd. (SMPL) delivers wholesale and institutional healthcare procurement, facilitating large-scale supply contracts for medical equipment, diagnostics, and clinical consumables.

  • Institutional procurement and bulk medical trade
  • Headquartered in Delhi with multi-state logistics
  • Comprehensive inventory spanning 40+ medical specialties

Dr. Smith Healthcare Solutions

Manufacturer & Global Exporter • Global Operations

Dr. Smith is the core manufacturing, export, and turnkey solutions arm, producing ISO/CE-certified hospital furniture, modular OT systems, medical gas pipelines, and anesthesia workstations worldwide.

  • Direct manufacturing with custom engineering
  • Global export footprint across Asia, Middle East & Africa
  • 1 Year comprehensive free product service & warranty

MediAid Curatives India Pvt. Ltd.

Patient Assistance & Hospital Solutions • India & Global

MediAid Curatives India provides international patient support, clinical facilitation, medical tourism assistance, and specialized hospital infrastructure development solutions.

  • Domestic & international patient assistance and second opinions
  • Hospital construction and turnkey clinical advisory
  • Medical tourism coordination and post-discharge home care

Synergy That Powers Complete Healthcare Delivery

By integrating manufacturing (Dr. Smith), wholesale trade (SMPL), surgical distribution (Surgihub), and patient services (MediAid), our group provides an unmatched ecosystem from factory floor to patient bedside.
