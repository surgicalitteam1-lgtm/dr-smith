Our Legacy & Operations

6 Decades of Trust, Quality & Innovation

Dr. Smith operates under the guidance of a Jalandhar-based parent company with more than 60 years of experience in trading, importing and exporting medical instruments and equipment.

As an integrated manufacturer, exporter, and supplier, we provide hospital furniture, advanced medical devices, surgical instruments, diagnostic equipment, and complete turnkey hospital projects worldwide.

Every product is manufactured to rigorous technical standards and customized to clients’ clinical needs. We back our craftsmanship with one year of comprehensive free service and round-the-clock technical support.
