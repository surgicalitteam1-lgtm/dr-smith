Corporate Profile

Surgical Wholesale Mart Pvt. Ltd.

Surgical Wholesale Mart Pvt. Ltd. (Dr. Smith) is one of the renowned healthcare enterprises in India, providing complete end-to-end solutions for hospital construction, modular operation theatres, medical gas pipelines, and over 50 specialized categories of hospital furniture and clinical devices.

We are certified under ISO 9001:2015, ISO 13485:2016, ISO 14001:2015, OHSAS 45001:2018, BIFMA, GMP, CE, and US FDA conformity. We manufacture products tailored to evolving clinical trends and back every installation with our signature one-year complimentary service warranty.
