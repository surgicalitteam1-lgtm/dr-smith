Corporate Overview & Strategic Vision

Dr. Smith (Surgical Wholesale Mart Pvt. Ltd.) operates under the guidance of a Jalandhar-based parent company with more than 60 years of experience in trading, importing and exporting medical instruments and equipment.

As a manufacturer, exporter and supplier, the company provides hospital furniture, medical equipment, surgical and laboratory instruments, and turnkey hospital solutions.

Products are manufactured to customers’ needs and specifications, with one year of free service provided for products purchased.
