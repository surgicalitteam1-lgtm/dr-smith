Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our turnkey hospital capabilities, modular OT, and global procurement.

Dr. Smith is an end-to-end partner for complete hospital construction and infrastructure: NABH/JCI compliant hospital architectural design, civil construction, MEP services, prefabricated modular operation theatres (OT), medical gas pipeline systems (MGPS), ICU setup, and full equipment outfitting.

Our company is certified under ISO 9001:2015, ISO 13485:2016 (Medical Devices Quality Management System), ISO 14001:2015, OHSAS 45001:2018, BIFMA, GMP, CE, and US FDA conformity benchmarks.

Dr. Smith equipped a landmark 500-bedded government hospital facility inaugurated by the Home Minister of India (Mr. Amit Shah), installed 125+ ICU ventilators for The Art of Living Organization, and supplies government health ministries internationally, including the Ministry of Health & Child Care, Zimbabwe.

Our Corporate Global Head Office is located at Plot No. 75/02, Shri Sai Complex, Ground Floor, Kapashera Border, New Delhi – 110037, India. Our primary manufacturing plant is at METL, Jhajjar, Haryana. Our Overseas International Head Office is at Q4-223, SAIF Zone, Sharjah, United Arab Emirates.
